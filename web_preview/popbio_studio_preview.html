      PopBio Studio v1.2.0 — Controls, Worksets & FASTA Format
      
    

    
This interactive Canvas workstation mirrors the native safe-Rust popbio-studio v1.1.0 architecture across all 17 organismal, theoretical, and empirical sequence modules.

    
    
      
  • Space: Play / Pause the active dynamical or genetic simulator.
    • 
      
  • Next Growth Phase: Advances time automatically until the biological phase transitions (e.g., Lag → Exponential → Diauxic → Stationary → Senescence in E. coli).
    • 
      
  • F5 / F9 & .popworkset: Save or restore complete multi-module checkpoints including RNG seeds, parameters, and custom FASTA alignments.
    • 
      
  • Empirical Deme Tags: Format FASTA headers as >Sample01|IsleRoyale or >Sample01 [Pop=North] to color Haplotype Network pie slices and compute Hudson F_ST / D_XY.
